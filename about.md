# About AI.WELLLabs

The Digital Futures initiative at WELL Labs is focused on 5 themes. These include the  Access, Availability, and Aggregation of data on the one hand, and digital tools that enable Analysis and Anticipation.

ai.welllabs.org is a destination where we intend to publish all of these data products and digital tools. 

We will be publishing datasets, GIS-based tools, productised AI pipelines, and modeling engines. As with all of the work that we do at WELL Labs, the starting point will be water, but that will by no means be the end. 

All of our data and tools will be open source and free to use. 

Our ambition is to provide more agency to those working in the ecosystem, especially to those who are working in the field. That is the only way all this work can move the needle for the sector.
